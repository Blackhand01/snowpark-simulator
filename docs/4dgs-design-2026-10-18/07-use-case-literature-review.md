# Use-case review before each optimization step

18 October 2026. Design only. No candidate below is selected for execution. This supplements the [sequential protocol](06-sequential-optimization.md): literature-based selection precedes each single-factor implementation, and every result retains comparisons with B0 and the accepted parent.

## Mandatory selection gate

For the chosen phase, prepare a [stage review](templates/stage-review.md): observed bottleneck or missing measurement; snowpark-specific hypothesis; unchanged-parent control; credible alternatives; primary paper/section/code evidence; allowed inputs and priors; complete resource/license/maintenance costs; incompatibilities; one discriminating experiment and rejection rules. Compare at least two plausible alternatives where available, including a simple engineering option. If none is adequately supported, record the search gap instead of inventing a SOTA recommendation.

Status vocabulary: DOCUMENTED PRINCIPLE, CANDIDATE, NEEDS SOURCE/CODE AUDIT, BLOCKED DEPENDENCY, or LOCALLY VALIDATED. No new method is LOCALLY VALIDATED here. The matrix below is an initial applicability screen, not completed implementation due diligence for every paper. Before each assignment, refresh only its relevant evidence and dependencies, then request human selection. P01 can be reused for this preparation; no extra agent role is needed.

## Scene implications

The verified pilot has fixed calibrated cameras, a large stationary environment and a small moving athlete/equipment region; see [baseline evidence](01-baseline-audit.md). Its original initialization is random: eliminating COLMAP cannot save a cost absent from B0. Static geometry and time-invariant pixels are different: rail occlusion, moving cast shadows and disocclusion can change an image even when the rail and snow do not move. Body, skis and clothing also need not share the same motion model.

**Design inference:** avoid assigning time-dependent parameters and optimizer state to genuinely static geometry where separation is reliable. Retain joint depth-aware composition until an alternative proves equivalent for rail-front/rail-behind views. Measure the dynamic Gaussian fraction and raster/optimizer cost; a small foreground pixel fraction is not a measured runtime fraction or a promised speedup.

## DSD-GS: focused candidate assessment

Fresh primary reading: [DSD-GS v1, §4.1–4.4, Eq.18, Appendix B.2, C.2 and E.2–E.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.30863v1). The local [p0033 note](/Users/stefanoroybisignano/Desktop/GaussianSplatting/Thesis/references/papers/notes/dsd-gs.md) was read; an eight-second extraction attempt on its FileProvider PDF timed out. No fresh local PDF-content or hash validation is claimed.

**Paper facts:** DepthSplat initialization and FlowFormer motion estimates separate static/dynamic primitives. Static parameters are frozen during dynamic fitting; cached static RGB is composited as `C_dynamic + T_dynamic*C_static`. Other components include reduced static color order and an edge-based density limit. Appendix E.3 acknowledges blur under substantial motion. These components are not one isolated optimization. The [official project](https://young0tete.github.io/dsd-gs/) advertises approximately 10-minute fitting and over 700 fps at 1352×1014 on RTX 5090; these are author results, not M1/free-Colab estimates. The project currently labels code “TBA.”

**Our applicability assessment, not an author claim:** a dynamic-over-static image overlay cannot generally place an opaque rail in front of an overlapping skier. Reusing static geometry is therefore a higher-confidence design direction than copying this compositing shortcut. A depth-aware replacement would be our adaptation, with different costs and no entitlement to the reported speedup. Novel interactive camera poses also invalidate a per-view image cache. Thin skis, weak snow texture, shadow variation and a fast jump are explicit stress cases, not demonstrated failures of this paper on our scene.

**Inputs and readiness:** calibrated training RGB/time plus approved pretrained priors imply I1. Original Blender masks, depth or rig would instead create an assisted protocol. [FlowFormer official code](https://github.com/drinkingcoder/FlowFormer-Official) declares Apache-2.0 and provides model links; complete weight/subdependency rights remain to be checked. DepthSplat license/checkpoint closure was not completed; attempted repository pages were unavailable through the browser. No DSD-GS implementation commit, runnable environment, complete license chain or independent reproduction was verified. Peak full-pipeline host/device memory, download/setup cost, M1 playback cost and reproducible export remain unresolved. Keep the exact version/weights as an unresolved dependency card until verified; do not silently choose substitutes.

**Decision:** high-priority source for static/dynamic separation and reuse; full backend adoption is blocked pending code/dependency audit. Do not bundle initialization, split/freeze, motion representation, density policy, color order and cache into one claimed component experiment. A full reproduction, if later feasible and approved, is a package-level comparison only.

## Closely related alternative

[Swift4D paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.12307) learns which primitives need motion and uses a compact deformation representation. Its [official implementation](https://github.com/WuJH2001/Swift4d) is available, requires CUDA rasterization, simple-knn and tiny-cuda-nn, and documents shorter chunks for its large-motion dataset. The [renderer source](https://github.com/WuJH2001/Swift4d/blob/main/gaussian_renderer/__init__.py) retains noncommercial research/evaluation terms. Full dependency licensing, snowpark-compatible initialization, chunk-boundary behavior and resource costs remain unaudited. This is a credible alternative to investigate, not an approved installation or a prediction that the jump needs the same chunk size.

HUGS, GauHuman, GART, SC-GS, EDGS and the other works below retain the evidence and limitations in the [literature cards](02-literature-and-codesign.md). They were not all re-read or newly benchmarked during this focused revision.

## Recommended next documentary decisions

1. Complete per-stage evidence gaps, without running the designed measurements. Separate measured costs from hypotheses.
2. For representation, shortlist minimal split/joint rendering, Swift4D, and DSD-GS-inspired components; keep the full DSD-GS package blocked until reproducibility dependencies exist. These are implementations of the existing architecture family, not three additional architectures.
3. For a first reconstruction trial, retain the ordinary longer-training control. Foreground weighting is a plausible inexpensive initial factor; static/dynamic decomposition is the strongest scene-specific architectural hypothesis. Their actual execution order awaits evidence and user approval.
4. Populate the stage-review template before selecting each subsequent factor. Require primary-source refresh and an explanation of why its assumptions fit snow, rail occlusion, jump duration, available views and hardware.
5. After each independently approved experiment, compare with B0 and the accepted parent and stop for human review. Do not combine individually reported paper gains or treat this shortlist as authorization.
